# Pre-registration — conviction + coverage as a buy-signal filter

**PROCESS DEVIATION, DISCLOSED FIRST.** The thresholds below (conviction > 60,
coverage > 80%) were proposed by the user and I measured them on the existing
forward record *before* writing this file. So the numbers in §2 are a
**hypothesis generated from data already seen**, not a result. They cannot be
their own confirmation. This document exists to fix what *would* confirm them,
and it is not binding by construction — same disclosure as
`PREREG_2026-07-29.md`, and for the same reason: recording the error is
cheaper than pretending it did not happen.

---

## 1. The proposal

Fire buy signals only when the alert-time conviction score is above 60 **and**
dimension coverage is above 80%. Everything else is logged but not treated as
actionable.

## 2. What the forward record currently shows

`journal/journal_outcomes.csv`, buy-type alerts with a `plan_followed_R` and a
frozen alert-time conviction, as of 2026-08-13 (n = 274):

| cohort | n | expectancy | median | win% | stopped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all buy signals (baseline) | 274 | +0.150R | −0.033 | 48% | 50 |
| conviction > 60 | 111 | +0.318R | +0.032 | 51% | 13 |
| coverage > 80% | 210 | +0.194R | +0.017 | 50% | 31 |
| **both** | **101** | **+0.377R** | +0.065 | 52% | **10** |
| the excluded remainder | 173 | +0.017R | −0.074 | 46% | 40 |

Conviction bands are **monotonic**, which no rejected overlay has ever been:

| band | n | expectancy |
|---|---|---|
| 0–50 | 66 | −0.101R |
| 50–60 | 95 | +0.143R |
| 60–70 | 94 | +0.298R |
| 70–80 | 16 | +0.496R |

## 3. Three reasons this is not yet a result

**(a) 82% of the cohort is still open, at a median age of 18 days.** 224 of 274
trades have not resolved. Early unrealized R is dominated by which names have
moved *so far*, and high-conviction names are high-RS names — momentum shows up
in unrealized R first and is exactly what mean-reverts later. The canonical
baseline is +1.67R over full holds; +0.377R at 18 days is not the same
measurement.

**(b) The prior evidence points the other way.** Matrix v1 already tested
gating entries on point-in-time fundamentals — configs `B1_pit_gate_failopen`,
`B2_pit_gate_failclosed`, `B3_pit_gate_050` — and found **monotonic
degradation**: +0.39R, +0.39R, +0.54R against a +1.27R ungated baseline. The
mechanism found then was that price leads reported fundamentals at inflections,
so the gate blocks the explosive early turnarounds. A conviction gate is a
fundamentals gate plus a momentum term, and the momentum term is already in the
entry — so the marginal content of this filter is the part that was rejected.

**(c) The effect is conditional, not general.** Splitting by RS:

| | n | expectancy |
|---|---|---|
| RS ≥ 80, passes filter | 73 | **+0.604R** |
| RS ≥ 80, fails filter | 98 | +0.064R |
| RS < 80, passes filter | 28 | **−0.214R** |
| RS < 80, fails filter | 73 | −0.017R |

Among already-strong names the filter separates cleanly. Among weaker names it
is **worse than no filter at all**. That is an interaction, not a main effect,
and a rule adopted on the pooled number would be adopted for the wrong reason.

## 4. Decision rule — fixed here, evaluated later

The filter is adopted as a **capital-bearing gate** only if, on a cohort that
did not exist when this file was written:

1. **n ≥ 120** buy-type signals with a frozen alert-time conviction, **and**
2. **≥ 60% of them closed** (stopped, or exited by the two-lot rules) — the
   open-trade contamination in §3(a) has to be gone, and
3. expectancy of the passing cohort exceeds the full baseline by **≥ +0.25R**,
   and
4. the relationship stays **monotonic across all four conviction bands**, and
5. it holds **within the RS ≥ 80 stratum AND does not invert** in RS < 80 —
   i.e. the RS < 80 passing cohort is not worse than its own control.

Failing 5 while passing 1–4 means the filter is a momentum interaction and is
adopted, if at all, only as `RS ≥ 80 AND conviction > 60 AND coverage > 80%`,
which is a **different rule** and needs its own registration.

**Anything less than all five: not adopted.** It stays what it is today — a
label on the card and a column in the forward record.

## 5. What cannot be tested and why

A full conviction backtest is **impossible**, and that limit should be stated
rather than worked around. Two of the eight dimensions — `theme_tailwind` and
`catalyst` — are news-derived, and no historical news corpus exists for this
universe. Replaying them would require knowing what was published on each past
date, which the project has never had.

So the only backtestable approximation is a six-dimension conviction over
point-in-time fundamentals plus RS, renormalised — which is close to what
matrix v1's B configs already rejected. The genuinely new content of this
proposal (the news dimensions) is **forward-testable only**. That is the honest
reason this pre-registration sets a forward bar rather than commissioning
another matrix run.

## 6. Interim disposition

Unchanged. Entries stay 100% technical and full-universe. The conviction score
remains decision support on the card, and `conviction_score` / `coverage_pct`
continue to freeze into `journal/journal_outcomes.csv` at alert time so this
question stays answerable — which is the whole reason those columns exist.
