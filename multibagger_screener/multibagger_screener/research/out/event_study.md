# Multibagger event study — survivorship-free NSE panel

Universe: point-in-time, median traded value >= Rs 1.0 crore, price >= Rs 5, >= 250 sessions of history. One event per stock per 120 sessions. Entry = next session's open. Discovery ends 2015-12-31.

**Lift** = the signal's multibagger rate divided by the universe's rate on the SAME dates (so a bull market cannot flatter a signal). **Exit multiple** = enter at the next open, exit on a close below the 50-day average after 20 sessions or a -20% stop. **Earliness** = share of the (log) move from the prior-year low to the 1-year peak that was still ahead at entry, for the events that tripled.
