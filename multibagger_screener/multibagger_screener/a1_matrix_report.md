## Post-run disclosure (appended 2026-07-31, not generated by the script)

**The registered MAR bar of 3.58 was mis-specified.** It came from
VALIDATION_REPORT's breakout **+ EPISODIC PIVOT** figure, but this harness runs
breakouts only — `A1_control` reproduces at **MAR 2.69** (expectancy 1.667R,
matching the canonical lot-level number exactly). The bar was set on one
configuration and applied to another. It was **not** lowered after the fact.
Both readings are recorded, and both reject:

| reading | A1_a | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| rule as written, MAR >= 3.58 | 2.76 | FAIL |
| like-for-like vs control MAR 2.69 | +0.07 | inside noise |
| P2 chop cohort vs control 0.342 | **0.245** | **DEGRADES** |
| blended expectancy vs control 1.667R | 1.291R | dilutes |

**Dose-response is the decisive read.** Doubling sleeve risk (A1_c) collapses
MAR 2.76 -> **1.89** and breaches the drawdown bar at **-21.47%**. A real effect
does not invert when you double it. The script's `mono` check was too narrow to
catch this (it fired only if BOTH probes beat the primary); it has been
corrected for future runs and the defect is recorded here rather than hidden by
the fix.

**Cannibalisation confirmed as predicted.** Sharing slots (A1_b) drops breakout
expectancy 1.667R -> **1.397R** and sleeve expectancy 0.521R -> 0.131R. No
shared-slot variant may be adopted.

**What did reproduce:** the sleeve's standalone **0.521R** is consistent with
V3a's +0.41R, and with its own slots it barely disturbs the breakout book (96
breakout positions in both control and A1_a). The entry class is real. It is not
worth capital.

**Disposition: anticipation stays alerts-only, zero capital.** Second rejection
for capital purposes.
