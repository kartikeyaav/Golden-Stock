# Pre-registration — extension ruler (X1) and re-entry suppression (X2)

> **OUTCOME (2026-07-29): BOTH REJECTED, before implementation.**
> X1 inverts — distance above the 30-week MA, prior 6-month return and position
> in the 52-week range all correlate **positively** with forward R in the
> backtest (+0.214 / +0.158 / +0.223 pearson), and the mildest usable filter
> destroys 56–66% of all profit. X2 is a null — RE-ENTRY WINDOW (−0.188R,
> n=66) is indistinguishable from BUY CANDIDATE (−0.204R, n=64) in the live
> record. Neither reaches a config; nothing in the live tagger was touched.
> This is the 14th and 15th rejected overlay.

**PROCESS DEVIATION, DISCLOSED — this file was written AFTER the measurement
ran, not before.** Every other prereg in this project was committed first so the
commit history proves the order; this one was not, so the decision rules below
were written with the output already visible and are **not binding by
construction**. That is the exact error the discipline exists to prevent, and
recording it is cheaper than pretending otherwise.

Two things limit the damage, and neither is a substitute for having done it in
the right order. First, both proposals were REJECTED — the failure mode
post-hoc rules create is a rule tuned to admit a result, and nothing was
admitted. Second, the margins are not close to any threshold a reasonable rule
could have been drawn at: X1's sign is wrong on all three measures
simultaneously, and the cheapest usable filter costs 56% of all profit. No
pre-specified rule passes that.

If either proposal is ever reopened, it starts over with a file committed first.
Same escape hatch P5 used otherwise: a cheap measurement on data already on disk
can kill a premise before the matrix is ever run.

## Origin

HFCL and STLTECH sat at ranks #1 and #2 of the conviction shortlist (80.6 /
80.5, scored 2026-07-26) and fell 20.6% and 15.0% from their peaks over the
following four sessions. Diagnosis offered: both topped on their own Q1 FY27
results print after 192% and 412% six-month advances, at a *lower* high; the
score has no term for how much of the move has already happened; and
`is_extended` measures only against the 50-DMA (`pct_above_50dma > 25` or
`atr_above_50dma > 3.5`), so both read "not extended" at −3.71% and −5.75%
below their 50-DMA while sitting +51.7% and +59.9% above their 30-week MA.

**That diagnosis produced two proposals. Both are tested here, and the point of
testing them is that the diagnosis being *descriptively* true of two names says
nothing about whether the factor pays across the population.**

## X1 — does prior advance / extension predict worse forward R?

Measured on the canonical baseline's own saved trades,
`matrix_trades/SZ2_B_equity_cap15_r1.25.csv` (96 entries, 2023-08 → 2026-07,
equity-basis sizing, risk 1.25%, cap 15%, costs applied, expectancy +1.61R
blended). For each entry, `scoring.stage_tagger.classify_stage` is called on
the symbol's cache truncated to the entry date — the project's own function, so
the measurement and the live tagger cannot drift.

**Decision rule (written post-hoc — see the deviation note above):** X1 proceeds
to a pre-registered matrix only
if the relationship is *negative and monotonic* across at least two of the three
measures (`price_vs_30wma_pct`, prior 6m return, `pos52`). A negative
correlation in one measure alone, or a non-monotonic bucket profile, kills it —
the cost of a wrong entry filter in this system is the right tail, and a
27%-win-rate / 9.6:1-payoff strategy cannot afford a filter adopted on noise.

**Result: rejected — the sign is wrong on all three.**

| measure at entry | pearson vs blended R | bucket profile |
|---|---|---|
| % above 30-week MA | **+0.214** | most-extended buckets best (19.25R, 6.37R) |
| prior 6-month return | **+0.158** | worst bucket is the *modest* +25–60% run (−0.13R) |
| position in 52-week range | **+0.223** | monotonic **increasing**: 0.6–0.8 → −0.30R, 0.96+ → +5.16R |

Cost of the filter, had it been adopted:

| 30wMA extension filter | entries blocked | R lost | share of all profit | surviving expectancy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| block > +25% | 32 | 135.1 | **66%** | 0.31R |
| block > +40% | 10 | 115.2 | **56%** | 0.46R |
| block > +50% | 7 | 37.2 | 18% | 1.32R |
| *(no filter)* | — | — | — | **1.61R** |

The names a >+40% filter would have blocked, i.e. the answer to "would the long
ruler have cost you TARIL and CUPID":

| name | entry | above 30wMA | prior 6m | pos52 | blended R | core R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V2RETAIL | 2023-09-14 | +40.0% | +79% | **1.00** | **+80.07** | +147.13 |
| GVT&D | 2023-08-11 | +79.1% | +165% | **1.00** | +19.05 | +36.50 |
| CUPID | 2025-11-07 | +68.7% | +209% | 0.99 | +15.19 | +26.96 |
| SUZLON | 2023-10-20 | +90.8% | +309% | 0.97 | +4.67 | +6.65 |

Six of the eight largest winners in the whole backtest entered at `pos52 ≥ 0.91`
and the top two entered at `pos52 = 1.00` — at the 52-week high. The profile
diagnosed as the risk factor on HFCL/STLTECH is the profile of the system's
biggest winners. Same mechanism as the 13 prior rejections: price leads, strength
persists, and every "safety" filter removes the right tail the strategy lives on.

## X2 — is RE-ENTRY WINDOW the defective alert class?

The backtest cannot see this. `is_extended` appears **nowhere** in
`backtest/engine.py`; the engine enters on `trend_template_passed → vcp_valid →
breakout_today` and has no concept of either EXTENDED or RE-ENTRY WINDOW. Testing
X2 through the engine would have been a guard that cannot fire. Measured instead
on the live forward journal (140 buy-type signals, 130 with a plan-replayed R).

**Decision rule (written post-hoc — see the deviation note above):** X2 proceeds
only if RE-ENTRY WINDOW underperforms BUY CANDIDATE by ≥ 0.15R with n ≥ 40 on
both sides.

**Result: rejected — null.**

| alert kind | n | mean plan-replayed R |
|---|---|---|
| RE-ENTRY WINDOW | 66 | −0.188 |
| BUY CANDIDATE | 64 | −0.204 |

Difference 0.016R, wrong side of nothing. Re-entries are not the problem.

Extension is not a live risk factor either: `corr(above30w, R) = +0.010`
pearson / −0.038 spearman across the same 130 signals. Within the NO VCP BASE
cohort the *most* extended bucket is the best (+0.014 vs −0.420 for the modest
15–30% bucket). One cell orders the way the diagnosis predicted — AWAITING
TRIGGER by extension, 0.173 → 0.050 → −0.339 → −0.638 — on **n = 11/5/3/4**.
Four observations is the "eight coin flips" error P5 was killed to avoid, and it
is not adopted.

## What the measurement found instead

The discriminator in the live record is not extension and not alert kind. It is
whether the backtest's own trigger was present at all:

| entry fidelity | n | mean plan-replayed R |
|---|---|---|
| AWAITING TRIGGER (base, no breakout) | 23 | **−0.062** |
| NO VCP BASE (no base at all) | 107 | **−0.225** |
| VALIDATED (the +1.61R entry) | **0** | — |

**Zero of 140 live signals have ever been a validated entry**, and 76% have no
VCP base at all. HFCL's two signals were AWAITING TRIGGER (both stopped, −1.13R
and −1.10R plan-replayed); STLTECH's 07-24 signal was NO VCP BASE, the worst
cohort, and carried no stop at all (2.5×ATR = 15.4% against the 12% cap, so the
risk engine skipped sizing it).

V2RETAIL entered *further* into its advance than HFCL was — +40% above its
30-week MA at a fresh 52-week high — and returned +80R. It had a validated
breakout. HFCL did not. **Extension without a base is not the same risk factor
as extension, and the system already computes, labels and freezes that
distinction into `journal/entry_signals.csv` without acting on it.**

That is not a new finding. It is `AUDIT_2026-07-25.md` finding F1, unchanged and
now carrying 140 signals of evidence instead of 117: the nightly scan fires on
tag transitions (live expectancy **−0.20R**) while the entry the +1.61R evidence
is built on has never once fired live.

## Disposition

- X1: rejected, weight 0. `is_extended` is **not** changed.
- X2: rejected, null. The RE-ENTRY WINDOW class is **not** suppressed or downgraded.
- No new gate, no new signal, no threshold moved. Evidence lock intact.
- Reopening either requires new pre-registered evidence, per brief §2B.

The one item this measurement leaves standing is context-only and already
sanctioned: prior-advance and up/down-volume may be *displayed* on cards and
frozen into `entry_signals.csv` so a future cohort can be asked the question
properly — the same "measurable and deletable" treatment the news layer got.
Freezing a column is not weighting it.
