# System audit — 2026-07-25

Everything below was **measured**, not inferred. Scripts and raw counts are in
the appendix so any claim can be re-run. Findings are ordered by how much they
change what you should do.

## FINDING 1 (critical) — the nightly scan is structurally blind to ~73% of the entries the backtest validated

**The claim.** The +1.67R / 47% CAGR evidence comes from one specific entry:
a VCP pivot cleared on >=1.5x volume inside a passing trend template
(`backtest/engine.py: generate_signals`). The live scan alerts only on a
**tag transition** (`* -> CONFIRMED`). Those two events mostly do not coincide,
so most validated entries can never produce an alert.

**The measurements** (60-stock random sample of `universe.csv`, full cached
history, seed 7):

| Measurement | Result |
|---|---|
| Engine entry days found | **66** across 53 stocks over ~6.2 years |
| Rate | 0.20 per stock-year → **~1.7 per week** across a 611-name universe |
| Live tagger agrees it is a validated entry | **55 / 66 (83%)** — the labelling logic is sound |
| Of those 55, how many fell on a **tag-transition** day | **15 (27%)** |
| **Invisible to the alerting mechanism** | **40 (73%)** — the stock was already CONFIRMED the day before, so no transition, so no alert |

**Live corroboration** (journal through the 2026-07-24 cloud run): **117
buy-type alerts, zero labelled VALIDATED** — 93 `NO VCP BASE`, 24
`AWAITING TRIGGER` (`journal/entry_signals.csv`). Forward expectancy across
120 tracked signals is now **-0.20R** (it was +0.14R on 21 July — the cohort
is deteriorating, not maturing), against a backtest expectancy of **+1.67R**.

The system is live-trading a trend-following tag transition that no matrix
ever validated, while the entry class that *was* validated has never once
fired. Note this is not a small-sample excuse: 117 alerts is a substantial
sample of *the wrong signal*. The validated signal's sample is still zero.

**Why the frequency looks low but isn't.** Validated entries cluster hard by
regime — in the sample: 2021: 18, 2024: 16, 2023: 11, 2025: 5, 2022: 3,
2020: 2. A quiet fortnight is expected; twenty quiet days across 611 names is
the mechanism, not the weather.

**The fix, and why it is not a strategy change.** `tag_stock` already computes
`validated_entry` for **every** name every night — the data exists and is
thrown away for all but the transitioning names. The EPISODIC PIVOT class
solved exactly this shape of problem in July and is already wired correctly:
it fires as an **event**, idempotent via `state.ep_alerted = {symbol: bar_date}`,
not as a transition. Mirror it:

```
kind = "BUY TRIGGER"    fires when tag_result["validated_entry"] is True
                        for ANY watched name, transition or not
                        idempotent via state["entry_alerted"][sym] = bar_date
```

No new signal, no new gate, no threshold moved — this only makes an
already-validated entry *alertable*. Expected volume ~1.7/week.

**Recommendation:** implement, and keep `entry_signals.csv` labelling as-is so
the VALIDATED cohort can finally be compared against the NO-VCP cohort with
real data on both sides.

### 1b (secondary) — 11 of 66 engine entries the tagger refuses to label

Nine land on days the tagger calls **EXTENDED** (23–49% above the 50-DMA) and
two on a stage-3 day. The backtest **took** those trades; live would skip them.
That may well be the safer behaviour, but it is an untested deviation from the
validated rule set. At minimum it should be labelled ("engine entry, extended")
rather than silently differing.

## FINDING 2 (high) — the forward journal does not measure the same quantity as the backtest

`scripts/journal_outcomes.py` marks each signal to market with exactly two
outcomes: `open` or `stopped` (booked at exactly -1.0R). It never applies the
two-lot exits the backtested +1.67R was built on:

- partial at +2.5R, stop to breakeven at +1.5R
- trading lot trails the 50-DMA
- core lot exits on a weekly close below the 150-DMA

So a signal that ran to +8R and round-tripped shows its round-tripped number
forever; a winner is never booked. Separately, entry is taken at the **alert
night's close** — a price that was already unavailable when the alert was
written (the backtest's honest fill, stress-validated at +0.96R, is the next
session's open).

The result: **+0.14R forward vs +1.67R backtest is not a like-for-like
comparison**, and it is the comparison the brief nominates as the gate for
scaling real capital.

**Fix:** add a `plan_followed_R` column (two-lot exits, next-open fill)
alongside the raw mark-to-market, and label which is which in the Journal tab.

## FINDING 3 (medium) — gap-through stops are booked as exactly -1.0R

`journal_outcomes` sets `r_to_date = -1.0` whenever a low touched the stop,
regardless of a gap-down that would have filled well below it. The engine
already has a gap-aware stop mode (stress config S3). Small, but systematically
flattering, and it accumulates in exactly the statistic being used as evidence.

## FINDING 4 (medium) — the capture-recall headline flatters

`capture_audit_report.md` prints `Recall: 3/3 identified` for the 3m window
after excluding 4 of the 7 top movers as structurally ineligible
(NO STRUCTURE / TOO YOUNG / EXTENDED). The per-mover classification is honest
and useful; the ratio built on top of it is not. Report both: raw (3/7) and
eligible-only (3/3).

Also worth noting plainly: on the **1-week and 1-month** windows the screen
caught **0 of 7** top movers in each. That is consistent with the design (it
trades bases, not spikes) but it is the number a reader will ask about.

## FINDING 5 (medium — privacy, not correctness) — real holdings are published

`github.com/kartikeyaav/Golden-Stock` is public and the Pages site is public.
`holdings.csv` and `positions.csv` carry real symbols, quantities and entry
prices. Nightly `backup_push.py` keeps them current. This is a personal
financial disclosure, and it becomes a much bigger problem the moment the
playground adds other people's accounts.

**Options:** scrub both files from the repo (and history) and keep them local;
or make the repo private and publish only the built dashboard.

## FINDING 6 (low, but every rupee figure depends on it) — `RISK.capital` is still a placeholder

`config.RISK.capital = 1_000_000`. Every position value, "capital at risk",
paper-book P&L and dashboard rupee figure is computed off it. Until it matches
real account equity, those numbers are illustrative rather than actionable.

## FINDING 8 (structural, now partly addressed) — the universe cannot see the market's biggest movers

The watched universe is 651 index constituents; the capture audit ranks top
movers *within* that set, so movers outside it are invisible by construction.
The NSE cash market has **2,389 EQ-series securities**; roughly 1,700 of them
are outside the index universe.

The penny/nano screen added on 2026-07-25 (`scripts/penny_scan.py`) opens that
door deliberately and separately — research surface, zero capital, journaled.
See HANDOFF §3N.

## Appendix — how to re-run the Finding 1 measurements

The three scripts used live in the session scratchpad; the logic is small
enough to restate:

1. **Engine entry rate** — `backtest.engine.generate_signals(df, 1.0)` over
   each sampled symbol's full cached history; count `breakout_today`.
2. **Tagger agreement** — for each engine entry day `i`, call
   `scoring.stage_tagger.tag_stock(df.iloc[:i+1], bench)` and compare
   `validated_entry`.
3. **Transition visibility** — for each tagger-validated day, tag `df.iloc[:i]`
   as well; a transition exists only when the previous day's tag was not
   `CONFIRMED`.

Sample: `random.seed(7); random.sample(symbols, 60)`, symbols filtered to those
with a cached CSV and >=400 bars. Cache as of 2026-07-16.
