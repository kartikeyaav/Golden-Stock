# Pre-registration — the NSE coverage-gap cohort (`nse_gap`, 377 names)

**PROCESS DEVIATION, DISCLOSED FIRST.** The backtest in §3 was run *before*
this file was written, on 2019–2026 history the strategy has already seen. It
is therefore a **hypothesis generated from data already seen**, not a result,
and it cannot be its own confirmation. This document exists to fix what would
confirm or refute it *forward*. Same disclosure, and same reason, as
`PREREG_2026-07-29.md` and `PREREG_2026-08-13.md`: recording the error is
cheaper than pretending it did not happen.

---

## 1. The proposal

Add 377 NSE-listed companies to `universe.csv` under a fourth
`index_source = "nse_gap"`. They are the band that sat between the two
screens: too big for the penny screen's gates (price ≥ ₹100 **and** mcap ≥
₹1,000cr) and in no Nifty index, because Microcap250 has a hard 250-name cap
and Nifty membership needs F&O eligibility and listing history — not because
the businesses are worse. Metro Brands, Hatsun Agro, BASF India, Vinati
Organics, G R Infraprojects, Ingersoll Rand.

Nothing else changes. No new gate, no new entry class, no sizing change.
Entries stay technical and validated; the cohort only widens what is watched.

## 2. Why a cohort label and not a silent merge

The backtested gain is **diversification, not stock-picking** (§3). That claim
is only ever testable if the names stay separable in the forward record, so
`index_source` must survive into `journal/` joins forever. A merge that loses
the label destroys the ability to answer "was this a good idea?"

## 3. What the backtest showed (data already seen — NOT confirmation)

Same engine, costs and capital as `run_baseline_backtest.py`: neutral 0.6
fundamental, min score 0.55, ₹10,00,000, 0.15%/side, ≥300 rows.
Window 2019-01-01 → 2026-09-04 (7.67 yrs). Cache coverage: incumbent 610/650,
gap 367/377.

| run | names | pos | CAGR% | maxDD% | expR | CAGR/|maxDD| |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| incumbent | 610 | 218 | 19.63 | −21.13 | 1.633 | 0.929 |
| gap only | 367 | 190 | **7.14** | −17.01 | 0.531 | 0.420 |
| combined | 977 | 244 | **21.66** | **−18.60** | 1.752 | **1.165** |

**The gap cohort is a WORSE universe on its own** (7.14% vs 19.63%). The case
rests entirely on the combination: +2.03pp CAGR, 2.53pp shallower drawdown,
+25% risk-adjusted. Net positive in **6 of 8 years**, and counter-cyclical to
the incumbent in two — 2021 (incumbent +₹28k, gap +₹144k) and 2026
(incumbent **−₹131k**, gap **+₹168k**).

A same-day technical snapshot said the opposite — that the gap names were
*better* than the incumbents on Stage 2 share, VCP rate and trend template.
They are not. That snapshot would have justified adoption for the wrong reason.

## 4. What would CONFIRM this, forward

Measured on `journal/journal_outcomes.csv`, buy-type alerts carrying a
`plan_followed_R`, joined to `universe.csv` on `index_source`.

Review at the **earlier of 2027-03-31 or 40 resolved `nse_gap` buy signals.**

- **PRIMARY — the cohort is not a drag.** `nse_gap` expectancy ≥ **0.00R**
  over ≥ 40 resolved signals. The backtest's standalone blended expectancy was
  0.531R; requiring only ≥ 0 is deliberate, because the claim being tested is
  diversification, not that these names are good.
- **SECONDARY — the diversification is real.** In calendar months where the
  incumbent cohort's summed realised R is negative, the `nse_gap` cohort's
  summed R is positive in **≥ 40%** of those months. Chance alone, given the
  ~29% win rate, would not clear this reliably.

## 5. What would REFUTE it

- `nse_gap` expectancy **< −0.15R** over ≥ 40 resolved signals → **remove the
  cohort**, revert `build_universe.py`, keep the file for the record.
- Or the cohort clears §4 only because of one or two names: if excluding the
  single largest `plan_followed_R` contributor drops expectancy below 0.00R,
  the result is **not** confirmed and the review extends rather than passes.

## 6. The band was fixed BEFORE the backtest

mcap ₹1,000–25,000cr and median turnover ≥ ₹1cr/day were chosen on 2026-09-06
from the coverage census, *before* any backtest was run, and were **not tuned
afterwards**. The ₹25,000cr ceiling exists to exclude the Nifty 100 largecaps,
which a multibagger screener should not chase. No other band was tested.

## 7. Known limitations, stated now rather than discovered later

1. **Survivorship, and it is the big one.** The cohort was derived from
   *today's* NSE master, so any gap-band company that delisted or collapsed
   between 2019 and 2026 is invisible. This inflates §3, and probably more than
   it inflates the incumbent, because sub-index small caps fail more often than
   index members. It is unquantifiable here — the repo has historical
   constituent snapshots for the indices and nothing for this band. **This is
   the single strongest reason §3 is not confirmation.**
2. **No industry.** NSE publishes no macro-industry outside the index files, so
   the cohort carries a blank one. Checked, not assumed:
   `phase_c._theme_read` returns **0.3 — a score, not None** — for a name in no
   theme, so the 15-point `theme_tailwind` weight stays inside coverage and
   these names are mildly *penalised* rather than renormalised upward.
   `tests/test_gap_universe.py` pins that behaviour.
3. **Fat tails.** Top 3 names are 84% of the cohort's backtested P&L. The
   incumbent is comparably concentrated (top 3 = 60%, and its 2023 alone is 73%
   of its total), so this is the strategy's signature rather than a cohort
   defect — but it means far fewer effective independent observations than 190
   positions suggests.
4. **Operational cost.** The universe grows 58%, so nightly alert volume rises
   roughly in proportion (~19 → ~30) and the weekly screener.in scrape grows
   with the shortlist. Positions grew only 12% in the backtest — the slot
   constraint binds, so the book does not flood, but the reading does.

## 8. The cohort is FROZEN

`scripts/build_gap_universe.py` derives it and is run **deliberately at
review**, never on a cadence. `build_universe.py` merges the committed
`gap_universe.csv` and never rebuilds it: a pre-registered forward test cannot
measure a moving target, and a live index-fetch failure must never silently
redefine the cohort. Index names win on collision, so a gap name promoted at
the next rebalance is relabelled by its index and stops counting as this
experiment.
