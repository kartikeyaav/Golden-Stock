# Review — 2026-09-25: is Golden Stock working, and what changed

The question asked: analyse the whole application (code, strategy, UI,
execution) now that enough forward data has accumulated, give an honest
opinion, and correct, remove or enhance whatever needs it.

Everything below was measured on data already on disk (1,055 journaled
signals from 2026-07-07 to 2026-09-24, prices refreshed to 2026-09-25) or on
re-runs of the backtest engine. The scripts that produced each number are
listed in §8.

---

## 0. The short answer

1. **The system is not a money printer, and nothing in this market is.** It
   is a sound, disciplined breakout/momentum system with a real edge that is
   smaller and more execution-dependent than its headline claimed.
2. **The published headline was optimistic.** 54.5% a year at −15.2% came
   from the strongest 2.9-year window, with costs left out of the equity
   curve, and from a configuration the system does not run. The live rules,
   measured once, honestly, over 2020 → 2026: **45% a year with ideal fills,
   30% with realistic execution, drawdowns of 21–25%.**
3. **Survivorship bias is large.** Simply owning today's index members,
   equal-weight, returned **33% a year** since 2020; the real, investable
   Midcap-100 ETF returned 21%. Every backtest number here carries that
   tailwind. Relative comparisons are informative; absolute ones are not.
4. **A plain monthly momentum rotation did as well or better** on the same
   universe (44% a year at −23%, still 43.6% at stress-level costs). A 50/50
   blend with a well-executed breakout book had the best Sharpe of anything
   tested (1.79). This is the strongest "better strategy" candidate, and it
   directly covers the breakout system's blind spot (V-shaped recoveries).
5. **Execution is the single biggest lever.** Buying at the next morning's
   open instead of the breakout-day close cost **10.4 points a year** on its
   own. That is larger than every scoring layer combined.
6. **In the forward record, the setup is what works, not the score.** Alerts
   with a live VCP base beat the universe by **+13.8% over 40 sessions**
   (75% of them did); uptrend-only alerts — 77% of everything the scan fires —
   by +3.8%. The 0–100 conviction score separates the bottom (under 50,
   including vetoes) and **does not rank anything above 50**.
7. **The AI layers are research, not selection.** Analyst BUY calls ran only
   slightly ahead of WAIT calls, and its SKIPs did not underperform. The
   paper book is up ~17% on a notional ₹10L while the universe was flat, but
   that mostly reflects which alerts reached the analyst (base-ready names
   first), not the verdicts.

## 1. The forward record (2026-07-07 → 2026-09-24)

### 1.1 Which alerts beat the market

Each buy alert measured from the **next session's open** against an
**equal-weight basket of the whole universe** over the same days, at fixed
horizons (so a rising market cannot flatter a label, and young and old alerts
are not mixed):

| alert type | alerts | 10 sessions | 20 sessions | 40 sessions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakout trigger fired (VALIDATED) | 11 | +3.3% | +6.3% (67% beat) | n=1 |
| Episodic pivot (gap) | 7 | +4.1% | +5.0% (80% beat) | n=1 |
| **Base ready, pivot not yet broken** | 126 | +2.0% | +5.2% | **+13.8% (75% beat, n=32)** |
| Uptrend only, no base | 612 | +0.3% | +1.3% | +3.8% (53% beat) |

The live, validated triggers are few (18 in 11 weeks), consistent with the
backtest's ~2.7 entries a month. The base is where the edge lives, which is
exactly what the backtest said. The "uptrend only" transitions are close to
noise and were most of what reached the phone and the Overview.

### 1.2 Did it find the stocks that ran?

Top 50 liquid gainers in the universe since 07-07 (+37% to +96%; median
liquid stock −2.6% over the same period):

- 30 of 50 got at least one alert — but typically mid-move: median **+12.7%
  left** after the first alert, of a median 48% run.
- 10 had a base label; the analyst said BUY on 7; the paper book held 3
  (WELCORP, SHILPAMED, SKYGOLD — all big winners there).
- **29 of the 50 were non-index names that were not scanned until 09-07.**
  The universe was the biggest single miss, and it has been fixed.
- The large misses with zero alerts (KALYANKJIL +62%, GMMPFAUDLR +96%,
  PCJEWELLER, REDINGTON) went from downtrend straight to "extended" within a
  few weeks and never passed through CONFIRMED. That is a structural blind
  spot of a base-breakout system for V-shaped recoveries — the backtest
  itself captured 0 of the month's top 50 in 68% of its months — and it is
  not something to "fix" by loosening the VCP rules. The momentum rotation
  in §3 is what catches that shape.

### 1.3 The conviction score

- On 642 alerts the composite has a weak positive relation to excess returns
  (ρ ≈ 0.08–0.11). By band, **under 50 (vetoes included) lagged the
  universe**; 50–60, 60–70 and 70+ were indistinguishable at 20 sessions.
- The August pre-registration (`PREREG_2026-08-13.md`) fixed a rule to test
  "conviction > 60 and coverage > 80%" on new data. On the 452 alerts since
  08-14 it fails: lift +0.05R against a required +0.25R, bands not monotonic
  (70+ was worse than 60–70). The monotonic pattern seen in August dissolved
  exactly as that document warned it might.
- By dimension (ρ vs excess return at 10 / 20 / 40 sessions):
  theme −0.07 / −0.05 / −0.13 · earnings +0.03 / −0.01 / −0.04 ·
  financial strength +0.09 / +0.08 / +0.26 · catalyst +0.08 / +0.10 / +0.11 ·
  RS & stage +0.02 / +0.06 / +0.05 (RS percentile's top third +10.7% vs
  +3.7% at 40 sessions).
- **35% of the weight sat on the two dimensions with no signal** (theme 15,
  earnings 20). The theme dimension also penalises the ~78% of names outside
  the curated theme map (0.30 for "no theme"), so it partly scored map
  membership rather than the company.

### 1.4 The AI layers

| analyst verdict | n | plan-followed R | raw return |
|---|---|---|---|
| BUY | 77 | +0.35R | +4.7% |
| WAIT | 52 | +0.19R | +2.1% |
| SKIP | 11 | +0.25R | +8.8% |

BUY/HIGH (n=6) did very well (+1.93R), but six trades prove nothing. The
analyst's research quality is visibly high (the memos catch real things —
SUPRIYA's NDPS probe, IKIO's untradeable stop), and that is its value. It is
not a demonstrated filter.

Paper book (every analyst BUY, mechanical exits): **+₹1.71L net (+17.1%)**
on the ₹10L notional, −₹60k realised from 11 stop-outs and +₹2.31L open,
while the universe was roughly flat. Caveat: it ran up to 29 positions (31%
heat) — more exposure than the 12-slot design — and most of its winners came
from the base-ready cohort that the analyst was fed first.

### 1.5 The capital gate

11 of 40 qualifying at a median age of 44 days, stop rate 23%. On 09-24 the
cohort averaged +0.38R against +0.30R required; after the 09-25 session it
averaged **+0.26R against +0.30R**, so a single down day took the quality
condition from passing to failing. With 11 trades the average moves that much
on one session, so neither reading is a verdict yet. Behind on count too: at
the measured 2.2 cohort signals per scanned week the 40 arrive around the
12-01 maturity cutoff with almost no margin. That is a frequency finding the
document already anticipates; do not move the bar.

## 3. Is there a better strategy?

**Yes, a combination — and it should be tested forward, not adopted today.**

| 2020 → 2026 | CAGR | max DD | Sharpe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50/50 realistic breakout book + momentum | 37.6% | −21.8% | 1.49 |
| 50/50 well-executed breakout book + momentum | 45.2% | −21.3% | **1.79** |

Daily-return correlation between the two is 0.70: related bets, but they win
in different years. Recommendations, in order of value per effort:

1. **Fix execution before adding anything.** The validated entry is a CLOSE
   above the pivot on ≥1.5× volume. Buying on the breakout day, near the
   close, is what the backtest measured; the nightly post-close alert forces
   a next-morning fill that costs ~10 points a year. The new *Ready to break
   out* list gives every base-ready name's pivot, the volume that confirms
   it, the stop and the quantity, with a "copy alert list" for Kite price
   alerts. Set the alert at the pivot, check volume around 3:15 PM, act
   before the close. (A GTT buy-stop would execute intraday on price alone —
   a different, untested entry that takes fakeouts; not recommended without
   its own test.)
2. **Pre-register a momentum core** (monthly, NSE-methodology score:
   volatility-adjusted 6- and 12-month momentum, top 20, hold until rank > 40,
   breadth rule for exposure) as one book with the breakout satellite — one
   combined output, not a competing scoreboard. Write the pass criteria
   before looking at forward data, exactly like the other pre-registrations.
3. **Stop adding scoring layers.** Thirteen overlays and now the conviction
   score have failed to improve entries. The evidence says the edge is in the
   setup, the exits and the execution.

## 4. The individual systems

| system | verdict | what changed |
|---|---|---|
| Stage tagger + VCP/EP triggers | The core. Works. | Nothing (evidence-locked). |
| Two-lot exits | Carries the right tail (avg win +7.6R vs avg loss −0.86R). | Position manager now replays missed sessions and fills gap-downs at the open. |
| Conviction score | Useful to rule out the bottom; does not rank. | Weights v2 (theme 15→5, earnings 20→15, financial strength 10→20, catalyst 10→15, RS 20→25, governance 8→5, smart money 12→10). UI shows it as a checklist and never as the headline. |
| Vetoes | Work: vetoed names lagged the universe at every horizon. | Nothing. |
| News radar / catalyst | Good risk watch and dossier; not a buy signal (positive news did not lead the trigger). | Moved to Research; Today shows only negative filings on paper-portfolio names or names about to be bought. |
| Bulk / block deals | New context layer. | NSE's nightly files archived forward (`data/deals.py`), same-session buy+sell churn set aside (88 of 96 rows on the first night); Research tab, stock page, setup chips. Measured forward before it earns any weight. |
| Phone digest | Had silently dropped most alerts since mid-August. | Parser fixed (1 of 25 lines → 25 of 25); adds a NEAR PIVOT block for buying on the breakout day. |
| Policy radar | Rare, bounded, well-built. | Its own Research tab with headlines and the uptrend names in each theme. |
| AI analyst | High research quality; weak as a filter. | Unchanged; shown as research. |
| AI committee | Research. | Unchanged. |
| Themes / sectors | Context (rejected as an entry gate). | Weight cut; Research tab. |
| Penny lab | Unvalidated research. | Unchanged, own page. |

**Volume, delivery and options data.** Volume already drives both validated
entries (1.5× on breakout, 3× on EP), and in the forward record a higher
breakout-day volume ratio did better (+3.1% → +6.4% by tercile at 20
sessions). Delivery % is already wired into the smart-money dimension.
**Options data is a poor fit for this universe**: only F&O names have it,
which excludes almost every micro and small cap where the multibaggers are,
and open-interest signals have no evidence here for multi-month trends. The
footprints of heavy buying that do exist for small caps are volume surges,
delivery %, bulk/block deals and promoter buying. Bulk/block deals are now
archived nightly (NSE publishes only the latest session, so the history
starts 2026-09-25). Promoter buying would need NSE's insider-trading API;
probed 2026-09-26, it answers without a browser session with a correctly
shaped but EMPTY result, which would read as "no insider buying anywhere" —
so it is deliberately not wired in.

## 5. What was corrected in code

**Measurement (AUDIT 2026-09-22 F3/F4/F5/F6, all reproduced first):**
- `backtest/engine.py`: `entry_day_stop` (an open fill is checked against its
  own bar), `cost_pct_per_side` (fees debited in the ledger, so CAGR and
  drawdown are net; the old path stays byte-identical at 0), and
  `week_end_flags` (a data cut ends a week only on a Friday).
- `scripts/position_manager.py`: replays every session since `last_checked`
  (a stop breached on a missed session is no longer lost), fills gap-downs at
  the open, and no longer lets a week that closed before the entry trigger a
  core-lot exit.
- `scripts/journal_outcomes.py`: the gate's ruler uses the entry-day stop.
  Cohort recomputed: one of 16 rows moved, mean +0.410R → +0.420R.
- `config.GATE`: benchmark corrected from MOM100 (Nifty Midcap 100 ETF) to
  **MIDSMALL**, the momentum-quality ETF the gate registered. Both
  amendments logged in `CAPITAL_GATE.md` §9.
- `config.EVIDENCE`: now the one honest run of the live configuration
  (feeds the landing page and the classic KPI strip).
- `scoring/regime.py` (AUDIT F9): an unknown regime now sizes at half risk,
  not full.

**Added 2026-09-26:**
- **The momentum core**, pre-registered (`PREREG_2026-09-25_momentum_core.md`)
  and running forward as a paper sleeve: `scoring/momentum.py` is the one
  scorer and rebalance shared with the backtest (the refactor reproduces the
  recorded 50.1% / −34.3% exactly), `scripts/momentum_core.py` runs nightly in
  the cloud. First signal 30 Sep, first fill 1 Oct at the open.
- **Bulk/block deals** as context (`data/deals.py`).
- **The phone digest parser**: every alert line with a `· conv` suffix had
  been dropped since mid-August — including two VALIDATED breakouts the gate
  counts (BALAMINES 08-24, BELRISE 09-18). Fixed, with a production-format test.
- **Personal holdings removed** at the user's request: holdings/positions
  files cleared, no dashboard builder reads them, the journal rows that
  managed them removed.

**Tests:** new `test_measurement_fixes.py`, `test_dashboard_v7.py`,
`test_momentum_core.py`, `test_near_pivot_digest.py`; each guard was made to
fail against the old behaviour first. Full suite green under both runners.

**Interface (v7):** see §6.

## 6. The interface

The v6 Overview was twelve panels long and led with things that cannot be
acted on (a nine-chip health strip, a scrolling tape, 22 "stage changes" of
which 20 were the near-noise uptrend-only kind, a "risk on your exposure"
list of 26 filings on names outside any book, and a backtest headline that
was not the live system). v7 is rebuilt around one question per screen:

- **Today** — market regime and position size, valid buy signals with the
  sized plan, the momentum core's status, the paper portfolio's due actions,
  the base-ready names nearest their pivot (with a copyable alert list),
  market pulse, and heads-ups only for names in a book or about to be bought.
- **Setups** — the breakout pipeline with a live chart beside the list;
  ↑/↓ flips charts, Enter opens the stock. (The Deepvue/MarketSurge workflow.)
- **Paper portfolio** — one book in two parts: the momentum core (holdings,
  NAV against the universe and MIDSMALL, the next rebalance preview) and the
  breakout book (every position's R, next rule and the price it fires at).
  Personal holdings are never shown.
- **Screener** — the universe, setup-first ordering, and a map view.
- **Research** — analyst memos, committee picks, themes, news, policy.
- **Track record** — the gate, the forward record against the universe, the
  honest backtest with growth-of-₹100 curves, the full signal log.
- **Penny lab**, **System** (health + the laptop run panel).
- A stock view with candles, 50/150-day averages, pivot / +5% buy zone /
  stop lines, alert markers, a relative-strength line with new-high markers,
  the plan, a business checklist, news, AI research and its history.
- Ctrl+K search, keyboard navigation, light and dark themes, phone layout,
  deep links (`dashboard.html#/stock/SYMBOL`).

Correctness fixed on the way: a gap-up whose stop had already been hit
(OPTIEMUS, 09-24) was still shown as a live buy. The classic page is kept at
`dashboard_classic.html` for a transition period.
