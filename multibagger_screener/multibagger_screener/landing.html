THE ONLY NUMBER THAT MATTERS

A threshold written down before the data arrived

"Wait and see how it does" is not a test — at +0.30R you can read it as nearly there or it failed, and both readings stay available forever. So the bar was fixed in advance, in public, with the anti-gaming clauses attached.

CONDITION 1 — EXPECTANCY

Half the backtest's own read, minimum

Measured like for like: a live signal 60 days old is held against what the backtest returned 60 days in, not against the full-hold number it only reaches a year later. A system delivering under half that is not the system that was validated.

CONDITION 2 — THE DUMB ALTERNATIVE

It must beat a momentum ETF

Mid/small-cap, momentum-ranked, quality-screened — the same factor exposure, one click, ~0.5% a year. If the ETF wins, the rational allocation is the ETF.

THE PART NOBODY ADVERTISES

Every idea that was tested and thrown away

Each of these was a plausible improvement, pre-registered, measured, and killed by its own number. They are kept on the record because a system that only publishes its wins has told you nothing.

REJECTED

Gating entries on fundamentals

Price leads reported fundamentals in this universe. Every fundamental filter on entries made the expectancy worse, not better.

REJECTED

Buying only in hot sectors

+0.22R gated at 40% sector heat, +0.11R at 60%, against +1.27R ungated — monotonically the wrong way. Sector heat is now a reading tab, not a filter.

REJECTED

Holding more positions

Expectancy fell from 1.29R to 0.89R as slots went 12 → 20. The queue already takes the best breakouts; extra slots only buy the weaker ones.

REJECTED

Progressive exposure

Scaling risk off the portfolio's own recent results cost 3.7–5.7 percentage points of CAGR. The market-regime rule already did that job.

REJECTED

News as a leading indicator

Names with positive filings went on to trigger 7.3% of the time against a 16.6% base rate. News stayed as context and risk, never as a signal.

REJECTED

Trading bigger

Raising risk per trade from 1.25% to 1.75% and 2.5% was measured twice. Both breached the drawdown limit and went negative in choppy conditions. Size stayed where the evidence put it.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Four things worth knowing

  • It is decision support, not advice. It hands you a researched, pre-sized plan. You place the order, you carry the risk, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy anything.
  • Plan against the stressed column. The historical universe excludes companies that later delisted, which flatters every backtest in this asset class — including this one. The stressed read exists so you never have to guess by how much.
  • Small-caps can trap you on the way out. Circuit bands and exchange surveillance can make an exit impossible at any price you planned. That is why exit risk is checked on every watched name, every night, and shown before you size anything.
  • Price decides; nothing else is allowed to. Fundamentals, news and the AI layers can veto a trade, shrink it or explain it. None of them has ever been permitted to create one — because when that was tested, it made the results worse.
